# Privacy

Onyx collects nothing. This holds for every copy of it: the Mac's, an iPhone's and an iPad's.

## Your notes

A vault is a folder of files on your device, and Onyx keeps no copy of it anywhere else. Nothing you write is sent to Alkaline Software or to anyone else. Onyx has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it carries no third party's code that does any of these.

A vault you keep in iCloud is synced by Apple, with your Apple Account, under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Onyx reads and writes the files; it does not see the account.

Spelling and grammar are checked on the device. English is checked by code inside Onyx, and on a Mac the other languages by the system's own checker.

## When Onyx uses the network

- **A link shown as a card.** When a note shows a web link as a card, Onyx reads that page for its title and picture, as a browser would. The site sees your device's address and that Onyx asked, as it would for any visit. What was read is kept on the device, and nothing is sent to anyone else.
- **Updates, on a Mac.** The Mac's copy asks GitHub whether a newer release exists and downloads it from there. GitHub sees your Mac's address and the version of Onyx that asked. The App Store keeps an iPhone's and an iPad's copy current, and that copy asks no one.

Onyx makes no other request.

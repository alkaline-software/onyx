# Probing

How to look at a state of the window from a script, without the person's hands.

## Rules

- Probe with a second copy, never the copy the person is using: `ONYX_HOME` at a scratch folder, and `ONYX_VAULT` at a vault made for the probe, never the person's.
- Under `ONYX_GO` a copy leaves nothing outside its window: a link or a file to open is said on stderr (`open …`) and not opened, what ⌘C or a menu's Copy would give is said (`copy "…"`) and the clipboard left alone, and what would go to the system's trash is said (`trash …`) and stays. A probe's note can hold any link, and a press that lands on the wrong row of a menu does no harm.
- A real click or key a script posts goes to the screen, not to the copy, and can land in the person's own windows even when the copy said it was in front. Use the `press=`, `key=` and `caret=` steps of `ONYX_GO`, which stay in the copy; `ONYX_TRACE` prints `mouse down` when a press reaches the editor.
- A system panel holds a copy open: with `print` or a save panel up, `ONYX_SHOT` never quits it, and it stays on the person's screen. Start the copy on its own, keep its own process id (not a pipeline's) and end it by that id, then check that none is left.
- The steps that are not presses or keys (`caret=`, `type=`, `live`, `source`) run as the copy starts, before the first layout and before any press, whatever their place in the list; to order a mode among presses, send its key (`key=ctrl-1`, `key=ctrl-2`). The mode is remembered in the copy's home, so a run says the mode it wants.
- The person's own pointer, passing over the copy's window, moves its pointer too: a `point=` or `pichover=` state can be gone by the picture.
- Say what was checked and what was not. A synthetic click does not reach a click handler in a background window, so a picture of the state a click leads to does not show the click works.

## A copy

```
cargo build -p onyx-app
ONYX_HOME=<scratch>/home ONYX_VAULT=<scratch>/vault ONYX_NOTE=<scratch>/vault/Welcome.md ONYX_GO=live ONYX_SHOT=<file.png> ./target/debug/Onyx
```

| Variable | What it does |
|---|---|
| `ONYX_HOME` | the copy's own settings and state |
| `ONYX_APPEARANCE` | `light` or `dark`, for this launch only; never written back |
| `ONYX_LANG` | a language's code (`de`, `zh-Hans`), over the system's |
| `ONYX_VAULT` | the vault to open, over the remembered one; nothing is written to the window's state about it |
| `ONYX_NOTE` | the note to open in it |
| `ONYX_SETTINGS` | open the settings on that section (`appearance`, `text`, `size` and `spelling` for those pages of Text, `about`) |
| `ONYX_GO` | steps, `;` between: `focus` (the keyboard in the note, which a synthetic press does not do in a background window, so the caret shows), `print` (⌘P; the panel is the system's and no picture of the window holds it), `cell=<row>,<col>` (the caret in that cell of the note's first table), `hover=<row>,<col>` (the pointer over it, so the handles show), `table=<what>,<row>,<col>` (a choice of a table's menu by its id, `row-below`, `col-right`, `align-right`, `sort-down`), `picture=<line>,<what>` (the picture on that line placed, `left`, `center`, `right`, sized, `auto` or `full`, given a width, or opened in the viewer, `view`, as its bar, its edge and its menu do), `pichover=<line>` (the pointer over that picture, so its edge takes a drag), `layout=<line>,<byte>,<what>` (the link at that byte of the line shown as `link`, `small`, `medium`, `large` or `preview`, as its menu does; a web card reads its page over the network into the copy's home), `point=<x>,<y>` (the pointer moved there in the window, nothing pressed), `wheel=<x>,<y>,<dy>` (a wheel's turn there, a gesture of its own, through the scroll router), `cmdwheel=<x>,<y>,<dy>` (the same with ⌘ held, which zooms a frame's PDF), `pinch=<x>,<y>,<d>` (a pinch there, 0.1 a tenth larger), `where` (the caret and the selection's other end, line and byte, and the note's scroll, on stderr), `type=<words>`, `text` (what the editor holds, on stderr), `tops` (where each line stands, on stderr, for measuring spacing; each takes a step of 400 ms, so a run of them also waits for the check), `updatefound` (a newer version waits: the gear's and About's dots; nothing is fetched), `note` (the vault's first note), `notes` (its first three, as tabs), `close` (⌘W: the tab showing, then the window), `hints` (the tabs' numbers, as ⌘ held shows them), `end` (the caret at the note's end, the view with it), `source` (the source pane shown or put away, as ⇧⌘S does), `srctype=<words>` (the keyboard in the source pane and those words typed there through its input handler, as keys are; the pane is made if it has not shown), `srcmenu=<x>,<y>` (a right click in the source pane at that place in the window, in its turn), `term` (the terminal shown or put away, as ⌃` does), `termtype:<words>` (those words typed in the terminal's shell, then Return), `termput:<words>` (the same without Return), `termcursor` (where the shell's cursor is, its size, where its typing begins and the screen's text, on stderr), `term:tab+` and `term:tab-` (a tab made, the one showing closed), `term:sel:<unit>,<line>,<col>,<line>,<col>` (a selection, 0 to 3 by the gap, letter, word or row, 4 more for a block; what it copies on stderr), `term:copied` (the toast), `term:clear` (⌘K); each terminal step lands in its turn, after the first drawing, `wait=<ms>` (that much longer before the next step in its turn), `flick=<x>,<y>,<to x>,<to y>,<dy>` (a gesture begun at the first place and its momentum carried to the second, ten events of `dy`, as a finger lifted and the pointer moved on: the scroller it began in moves and the one under the second place does not; `where` and `termcursor` say how far each went), `term:findopen` (the terminal's find card, as ⌘F there), `term:find:<words>` (those words in it, and how many it found on stderr), `term:findnext`, `term:mark:<text>` and `term:commit:<text>` (text an input method is composing, then typed, as gpui's input handler gives it), `sidebar`, `home` (the vault's page with the tabs left open), `scroll=<n>` (the note scrolled that far down, a big number for its end), `caret=<n>` (the caret to the start of that line, by the code the keys use, the view with it), `assets` (the sidebar's assets card open), `preview=<file>` (that file in a tab, as a press on it in the assets card opens it), `renamefile=<path>::<name>` (a file renamed as the rename sheet does it, an asset's links in every note with it, in its turn after a `preview=` before it; the vault must be one the probe can lose), `media` (what the preview's player has read of its file, a moment on: `media <ready state>,<width>,<height>,<length>,<error>` on stderr), `attach=<file>` (that file brought into the note at the caret, as a paste or a drop of it is: copied into the vault's `.assets` and referred to; the vault must be one the probe can lose), `press=<x>,<y>` (a left press and release at that place in the window, half a second on, sent to this window alone, so it goes through the hitboxes as a real one does, `press=<x>,<y>,<clicks>` for a double or triple click, `rpress=<x>,<y>` for the right button; presses and keys land apart, each on what the last drew, and the picture waits for them), `drag=<x>,<y>,<to x>,<to y>` (the same, moved before the release), `size=<w>,<h>` (the window to that size with no pointer in it, as a tiling key does), `sel` (what ⌘C would copy, on stderr), `key=<keystroke>` (a key as gpui names it, `enter`, `escape`, `cmd-a`, the same way; not typed text: a letter sent this way types nothing, in the note or in a field), `title=<name>` (the keyboard in the note's name and that name in it, not yet kept; a probe that keeps one needs a vault it can lose), `search` (the search palette with a phrase typed), `find` (the find bar over the note with a phrase typed), `find=<words>` (the same with those words), `menu` (the first note's menu), `rename` (its rename sheet), `vault` (the new-vault sheet; with `HOME` at a scratch folder, ↩ makes the vault in its `Documents`), `export` (the export sheet on the note showing), `pdf=<option>:<value>` (an option of the sheet as `Config::pdf` names it, `format:image`, `paper:a5`, `margins:large`, `size:12`, `landscape:on`), `pdfpage=<n>` (that page, from 0), `pdfto=<file>` (the file written there in the sheet's format with no save panel, as the panel's answer would be); any value keeps the window over the others |
| `ONYX_SHOT` | a picture of the window (the settings when they are open) after it is drawn, then quit; `ONYX_SHOT_FRAME=1` keeps the system's shadow and rim |
| `ONYX_TRACE` | prints, on stderr, when a key or a click arrives and when the frame after it begins, and what the editor's layout and paint cost, for finding where a wait is; `ONYX_GO=type` types a few words as the input method would |

Keys can be sent to a probe copy with `osascript -e 'tell application "System Events" to keystroke "ab"'` while it is in front, which is how the gap between a key and its frame was measured. Whether the source shows is remembered in the copy's home, and `source` turns it, so a probe that wants it says how it stands first or starts from a home without it. The terminal never shows at a launch. A process may take a picture of its own windows without Screen Recording access, which a terminal running a probe usually lacks.

## The core from a terminal

```
cargo run -p onyx-core -- paths | config | update
cargo run -p onyx-core -- vault <folder>
cargo run -p onyx-core -- vault <folder> new <name>
cargo run -p onyx-core -- --try-update <version> <copy of Onyx.app>
```

`vault <folder>` prints the tree as the window shows it, and how long it took to read, which is how a vault of thousands of notes is timed before anything is made faster.

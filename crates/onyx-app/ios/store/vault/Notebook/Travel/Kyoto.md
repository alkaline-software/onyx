## The plan

**Day one** is for walking: the Philosopher's Path in the morning, then lunch at the noodle place *by the canal*.

**Day two** starts early at the shrine on the hill, before its gates fill with people. The climb takes about two hours with stops.

> Leave the map at the inn for one afternoon and get lost on purpose.

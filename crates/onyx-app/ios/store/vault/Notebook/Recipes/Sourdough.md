One loaf, started the night before. The dough wants **time** more than work.

## Ingredients

| | Weight | Of the flour |
|---|---:|---:|
| Bread flour | 450 g | 90% |
| Whole wheat | 50 g | 10% |
| Water | 375 g | 75% |
| Starter | 100 g | 20% |
| Salt | 10 g | 2% |

## Steps

1. Mix the flours and the water. Rest for an hour.
2. Add the starter and the salt. Fold four times, half an hour apart.
3. Shape, then into the fridge overnight.
4. Bake in a covered pot at 240 °C for 25 minutes, then 20 more with the lid off.

> The loaf is done when it sounds hollow under a knuckle.
